# Business Workflows

## 1. Asset Owner Journey
1. Register (`/register`) & verify email (`/verify-email`).
2. Create or switch organization workspace (`/onboarding` or console switcher).
3. Submit digital asset (`/dashboard/owner/submit`) with repo URL or safe ZIP upload.
4. Run Technical & Commercial evaluation (`/dashboard/owner/analysis`).
5. Submit legal Ownership Declaration & evidence (`/dashboard/owner/verification`).
6. Specialist approves verification (`/dashboard/admin/verification`).
7. Publish Marketplace Listing (`/dashboard/owner/listings`).
8. Accept Buyer Inquiry (`/dashboard/owner/inquiries`) -> Deal Room opens (`/dashboard/deals/[id]`).
9. Negotiate offers, draft & sign agreement, wait for settlement, deliver handover (`/dashboard/deals/[id]`).

## 2. Buyer Journey
1. Register / Sign in -> Explore Marketplace (`/explore`) or Buyer Discover (`/dashboard/buyer/discover`).
2. Define Requirements (`/dashboard/buyer/requirements`) & inspect Explainable Matches (`/dashboard/buyer/recommendations`).
3. Save assets (`/dashboard/buyer/saved`) & compare side by side.
4. Submit Inquiry (`/assets/[id]`) -> Enter Deal Room (`/dashboard/deals/[id]`).
5. Propose/accept offer -> Sign agreement -> Initiate payment/settlement -> Confirm handover receipt.

## 3. Administrator & Specialist Journey
1. Sign in -> Admin Overview (`/dashboard/admin`).
2. Review Verification Queue (`/dashboard/admin/verification`) & Disputes (`/dashboard/admin/disputes`).
3. Moderate Marketplace listings (`/dashboard/admin/marketplace`).
4. Reconcile manual invoice settlements or process refunds (`/dashboard/admin/transactions`).
5. Monitor AI analysis jobs (`/dashboard/admin/analytics`), audit logs (`/dashboard/admin/security`), and commission rules (`/dashboard/admin/settings`).
