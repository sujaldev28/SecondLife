# SecondLife — Product Overview

**Tagline:** Give Abandoned Digital Assets a Second Life.

SecondLife is an AI-powered digital asset discovery, technical evaluation, ownership verification, and commercialization platform. It turns underutilized or dormant software projects, codebases, algorithms, developer tools, templates, and research prototypes into verified, commercially reusable digital assets.

## Core Stakeholders
1. **Digital Asset Owners** (individual creators, studios, startups, enterprises) seeking to monetize, license, transfer, or find revival partners for dormant software assets.
2. **Buyers & Acquirers** (developers, startups, technology companies, research institutions, enterprises) seeking verified reusable architectures, components, products, or IP to accelerate delivery.
3. **Evaluators & Verification Specialists** conducting technical due diligence, license/IP review, and ownership verification.
4. **Platform Administrators** governing compliance, marketplace quality, disputes, pricing/commission rules, and system security.

## Core Value Pillars
- **Controlled Discovery & Ingestion**: Safe intake via manual submission, permitted archive upload, or authorized GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket repository APIs, plus configurable inactivity-candidate discovery (never equating inactivity with abandonment or ownership).
- **Compliance-First Ownership & License Verification**: Structured ownership declarations, contributor disclosures, automated OSS license identification, evidence vaults, and mandatory human verification gates before publication.
- **Explainable Technical & Commercial Intelligence**: Deterministic static analysis, dependency/vulnerability inspection, secret detection, maintainability scoring, and transparent commercial revival/effort/opportunity modeling with documented assumptions and confidence bounds.
- **End-to-End Commercialization**: Structured inquiries, due diligence rooms, offer negotiation, licensing/transfer/revival agreements, payment & commission infrastructure (India-first Razorpay adapter + provider abstraction), and audited asset handover.
